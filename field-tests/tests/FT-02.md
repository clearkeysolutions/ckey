## What it checks

A 2-inch CKey reads from arm's length, about 60 cm.

## Why

Posters, shelf labels and screens are read from further away than a business card. Distance shrinks the code in the image exactly as print size does, so this shows how far the size results stretch.

## Set up

1. Print the test sheet at 100% scale and check that the code is 2.0 inches (51 mm) wide.
2. Tape it to a wall at eye level in even indoor light.
3. Mark distances of 15, 30, 60, 100 and 150 cm from the wall.

## Steps

1. Stand at each distance in turn, holding the phone square to the sheet.
2. Allow up to 5 seconds at each distance without zooming.
3. Record whether it read.

## What to record

- **Read within 5 seconds**, boolean, required. For each of: 15 cm, 30 cm, 60 cm, 100 cm, 150 cm.

Every report also records the device, operating system, reader and its version, and the printer or screen.

## How to grade it

- **Pass:** It read at 15, 30 and 60 cm.
- **Partial:** It read at 15 and 30 cm but not at 60 cm.
- **Fail:** It did not read at 30 cm.
