# FT-04: Lighting

## What it checks

A printed CKey reads in daylight, ordinary indoor light, dim light and under a reflection.

## Why

Low light adds noise and blur, warm light shifts colour, and glare washes out part of the code. The certification model includes noise and glare; this checks it against real rooms.

## Set up

1. Print the test sheet at 100% scale; the code is 2.0 inches wide.
2. Keep the phone about 25 cm from the sheet with the flash off.

## Steps

1. Try the code in each condition: outdoor daylight (not direct sun on the screen), ordinary indoor light, a dim room lit by one distant lamp, warm lamp light close by, and with a bright reflection crossing part of the code (glossy paper or a sheet protector under a lamp).
2. Allow up to 5 seconds in each.
3. Record whether it read, and optionally the light level from a light-meter app.

## What to record

- **Read within 5 seconds**, boolean, required. For each of: Daylight, Indoor, Dim, Warm lamp, Reflection.
- **Light level, if measured** (lux), number, optional. For each of: Daylight, Indoor, Dim, Warm lamp, Reflection.

Every report also records the device, operating system, reader and its version, and the printer or screen.

## How to grade it

- **Pass:** It read in daylight, indoor, dim and warm light.
- **Partial:** It read in daylight and indoor light only.
- **Fail:** It did not read in ordinary indoor light.
